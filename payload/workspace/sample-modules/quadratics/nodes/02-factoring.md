# 02 — Factoring

- Integer arithmetic (sums and products of integers)

## Enables
- `03 standard-form` (lets you see structure inside it)

## Sources

- Practice generator: any worksheet with monic and non-monic trinomials

## Fluency exercises

1. `x² + 5x + 6`
2. `x² - 7x + 12`
3. `x² + 2x - 15`
4. `x² - 9` (difference of squares)
5. `2x² + 7x + 3` (non-monic — leading coefficient ≠ 1)
6. `x² + 6x + 9` (perfect square trinomial)

## Schema-building exercises

1. **Why does factoring work?** Given `x² + 5x + 6 = (x + 2)(x + 3)`, explain why finding two numbers that **sum to 5 and multiply to 6** is the right strategy. Trace it back to FOIL.
2. **Difference of squares from geometry.** Sketch a square of side `a` with a smaller square of side `b` cut out of the corner. Show that the remaining area is both `a² - b²` and `(a + b)(a - b)`.
3. **When does factoring fail?** Try to factor `x² + x + 1` over the integers. Why can't you? What does this tell you about the roots?

## Metacog checks
- **Explanation**: Why must the two numbers sum to the middle coefficient and multiply to the constant?
- **Transfer**: Factor `x⁴ - 16` (apply difference of squares twice).
- **Connection**: How does the failure of `x² + x + 1` to factor connect forward to `07 quadratic-formula`?
